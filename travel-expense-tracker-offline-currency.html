Travel expense tracker with an offline currency converter

Abroad, you pay in one currency and think in another. G+Money logs each expense in the local currency and shows it in your home currency right away, and it keeps converting when you have no signal.

1. Currency conversion that keeps working offline

G+Money has 50 currencies built in. When you are online, rates update automatically. On a plane, in the subway or with roaming off, it keeps converting with the latest saved rate, or with a rate you set yourself (for example, the rate on your exchange receipt).

Note: Exchange rates are for reference; your card or exchange office may use a different rate.

2. Keep each trip separate

  • Create a trip or event with its own icon and color.
  • Tag each expense to a trip, or leave it unassigned for everyday spending at home.
  • Every trip gets its own subtotal in your home currency, with a running total as you go.
  • Log by category: food, transport, hotel, shopping, groceries, fees and more, or add your own with custom icons and colors.

3. A daily limit for your travel budget

Set a daily limit and see how much is left today. Filter by trip, category and date to see where the money went.

4. Export the trip when you get home

Export to text or CSV to share with travel companions or keep for your records. Summaries and exports are grouped by trip, with subtotals.

FAQ

Can I track travel expenses without internet?

Yes. Rates update when you are online; with no signal G+Money keeps converting with the latest saved rate or a rate you set yourself.

How many currencies does it support?

50 world currencies are built in.

Can I keep each trip separate?

Yes. Create a trip or event with its own icon and color, tag expenses to it, and see its own subtotal. Expenses without a trip count as everyday spending.

Can I set a daily travel budget?

Yes. Set a daily limit and the app shows how much is left today.

Can I export my trip expenses?

Yes, to text or CSV. Summaries and exports are grouped by trip, with subtotals.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Data stays on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud Sync.

Is there a subscription?

No. G+Money is a paid app with a one-time purchase; there is no subscription.

Is it on Android?

Yes. It is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.
