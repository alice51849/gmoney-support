FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I change my home currency?
Open Settings (top‑right gear) → Home currency, and pick from 50+ world currencies. Every total updates instantly.
Do exchange rates work without internet?
Yes. G+Money caches the latest rates so conversions keep working offline. You can also set a manual rate by tapping the rate bar on the Convert screen.
How do I set a daily budget / goal?
On the Convert screen, tap “Set daily goal” in the summary card and enter an amount. You'll see today's spending tracked against it, with how much is left.
Can I add my own expense categories?
Yes — on the Convert screen, tap the dashed “+ Add category” chip at the start of the category row (or add one in Settings). New categories appear first.
How do I export or back up my data?
In the Summary tab, use Export (text) or CSV to share your records. For a full backup, use Settings → Backup, and Restore to import it back.
Is there a subscription?
No. G+Money is a one‑time purchase — pay once and it's yours, with no subscriptions, no ads and no in‑app purchases.

🔒 Privacy first

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 June 2026

G+Money is built to be private by design. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What we collect

  • Nothing. G+Money has no account system and no sign‑up. We do not collect, transmit or sell any personal information.
  • Your expenses, rates, categories and settings are stored only on your device.

Network use

  • The only optional network request is fetching current exchange rates. This sends no personal data — just a request for public rate information.
  • With automatic rates turned off, the app makes no network requests at all.

No tracking

  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third‑party trackers or SDKs.
  • We have no way to identify you or your device.

Your data & control

  • You can export your data (text/CSV) or delete it any time from within the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children's privacy

  • G+Money does not collect data from anyone, including children.
