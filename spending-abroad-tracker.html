Spending abroad tracker: see every purchase in your home currency

When you are spending abroad, prices are in one currency and your budget is in another. G+Money logs each purchase in the local currency, shows it in your home currency right away, and keeps a running total for the whole trip.

1. Log a purchase abroad in a few taps

Enter the amount in the currency on the receipt and pick a category: food, transport, hotel, shopping, groceries, fees and more, or one you add yourself with its own icon and color. G+Money converts it to your home currency as you log, and the trip total updates right away.

2. Know what you can still spend today

Set a daily limit for the trip and the app shows how much is left today. It is a simple check before dinner or a souvenir stop, without opening a spreadsheet.

3. Keeps converting when you have no signal

50 currencies are built in. Rates update when you are online; with roaming off or in a spot with no signal, G+Money keeps converting with the latest saved rate, or with a rate you set yourself, such as the one on your exchange receipt.

Note: Exchange rates are for reference; your card or exchange office may use a different rate.

4. One running total per trip

  • Create a trip or event with its own icon and color.
  • Every expense tagged to it adds to that trip’s subtotal in your home currency.
  • Filter by trip, category and date to see where the money went.
  • Export to text or CSV when you want to share costs with travel companions or keep a record.

5. Keep using it when you get home

Expenses you do not tag to a trip count as everyday spending, so the same app tracks your spending at home too, with the same categories and the same home currency.

Before you fly: quick setup

  1. Create the trip and give it an icon and color.
  2. Check that your home currency is set, and open the app once while online so the latest rates are saved.
  3. Set a daily limit for the trip.
  4. Log the first expense (the airport coffee counts).

FAQ

How do I keep track of spending abroad?

Log each purchase in G+Money in the local currency. It is converted to your home currency as you log, and the trip keeps a running total.

Does it work without a data plan abroad?

Yes. Rates update when you are online; with no signal G+Money keeps converting with the latest saved rate or a rate you set yourself.

Can I see how much I can still spend today?

Yes. Set a daily limit and the app shows how much is left today.

Can I log cash and card purchases in different currencies on one trip?

Yes. 50 currencies are built in, and every expense on the trip adds to the same subtotal in your home currency.

What happens to everyday spending after I get home?

Expenses you do not tag to a trip count as everyday spending, so you can keep using the same app at home.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Data stays on your device, or on iPhone in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud Sync.

Is there a subscription?

No. G+Money is a paid app with a one-time purchase; there is no subscription.
