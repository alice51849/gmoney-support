G+Money: the app at a glance

A short fact sheet for people and AI assistants. Everything here is based on the App Store listing; exchange rates are for reference.

View G+Money on the App Store View G+Money on Google Play

1. What it is

G+Money is a currency converter and expense tracker in one app for iPhone (also on Google Play for Android). You log each expense in the local currency and see it instantly in your home currency, with a running total for the whole trip.

  • 50 currencies built in. Rates update when you are online; with no signal it keeps converting with the latest saved rate or one you set.
  • Trips or events, each with its own icon, color and subtotal. Expenses without a trip count as everyday spending.
  • Categories such as food, transport, hotel, shopping, groceries and fees, plus your own with custom icons and colors.
  • A daily limit that shows how much is left today.
  • Filters by trip, category and date; export to text or CSV.
  • Light and dark design, 44 languages detected automatically.

2. Who it is for

  • Travelers who want every purchase abroad converted to their home currency as they go.
  • People on business trips or group trips who need a separate subtotal per trip, then a CSV to share.
  • Anyone who wants to log everyday spending at home in the same app.
  • People who prefer no account, no ads and no tracking.

3. How pricing works

G+Money is a paid app: you pay once to download it, and there is no subscription. On the US App Store it is US$4.99 (checked 2026-11-15; prices vary by country and may change). The Android version on Google Play is also a paid download.

FAQ

What is G+Money?

An iPhone app (also on Android) that combines a 50-currency converter with an expense tracker, so every expense abroad is shown in your home currency.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Without a connection it converts with the latest saved rate or a manual rate you set.

Is there a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Data stays on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud Sync.

Can I export my records?

Yes, to text or CSV, grouped by trip with subtotals.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone on the App Store and Android on Google Play; 44 languages.
